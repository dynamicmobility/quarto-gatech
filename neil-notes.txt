

Overview:
This paper shows that "muscle synergy priors" enhance biomechanical fidelity, specifically in muscoluskeletal simulation. The paper extracts low-dimensional muscle synergy basis from inverse musculoskeletal analyses of overground walking trials. They then create a 3-dimensional muscle driven model and train it across various speeds and uneven terrain using RL. Overall, the paper shows that embedding neurophysiological structure in RL can improve biomechanical fidelity.
